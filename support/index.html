Answers to the questions players ask most about Restock Rush. If yours isn't here, email us and a real person will reply.

We read every message and reply as soon as we can. It helps a lot if you include your phone model, your Android version, and your Player ID from Settings > Privacy choices in the game.

Restock Rush

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone. You don't need an account to play, and we never ask for your name or email. That also means uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes your progress, so please keep that in mind before you reinstall or switch phones.

I bought "Remove ads" but I'm seeing ads again

Purchases are tied to your Google account, so they come back automatically once you're signed in to Google Play with the account you bought with. Close and reopen the game to restore them. If they still don't come back, email us with your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.) and we'll sort it out.

How do I get a refund?

Google Play handles payments, so refunds go through Google. Within 48 hours of buying you can request one from your Google Play order history; after that, follow Google's refund instructions. You can also email us and we'll help.

When do ads appear?

Only between levels, and optional reward ads you choose to watch (for example to revive). There are never ads in the middle of a level.

The game crashed or something looks wrong

Sorry about that. Email us what you were doing when it happened, the level number, your phone model and your Android version. A screenshot or short screen recording helps us find it much faster.

Privacy and your data

How do I ask you to delete my data?
  1. Open Restock Rush, go to Settings > Privacy choices, and copy your Player ID.
  2. Email it to support@highwaterdesigns.com with the subject "Data request" and tell us what you'd like us to do.

We answer within 30 days. Uninstalling the game deletes your local save. The privacy policy explains your rights in full.

How do I turn off personalised ads?

Go to Settings > Privacy choices in the game. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads on most Android phones).
