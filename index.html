How we work

Three rules for every game we ship

01 Fair play

No ads in the middle of a level, no energy meters that stop you playing, and the rules are the same for everyone whether they pay or not.

02 Short sessions

A level takes a couple of minutes. You can put the phone down at any time and pick up exactly where you left off.

03 Made by people

Our games use art, animation and sound made by working artists and recorded by sound designers, licensed properly and put together by hand.
