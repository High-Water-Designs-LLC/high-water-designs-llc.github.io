Short description

Restock Rush is a quick, satisfying shop sorting puzzle. Fresh stock rolls in on the delivery belt. Drag or tap each item onto a shelf, line up three of a kind to clear them, and hand the goods to the customers waiting at your counter.

About the game

Restock Rush puts you behind the counter of a small shop that never stops getting deliveries. Every level is a puzzle about space: a cluttered shelf jams fast and the belt keeps rolling, so you think a few moves ahead to keep goods moving and customers happy.

Clear items back to back to build a Rush combo and multiply the tips your customers leave. Earn up to three stars on every level and spend them renovating your shop. Locked shelves, mystery crates and VIP customers keep each store fresh.

The game is fair by design: no ads in the middle of a level, no level timer and no energy system. Every item has its own shape and symbol, never colour alone, so it plays well for colourblind players.

Key features

  • Sort the delivery belt, match three to clear, and fill customer orders.
  • Rush combos that multiply customer tips.
  • Renovate your shop with stars you earn.
  • No mid-level ads, no level timer, no energy system.
  • Colourblind friendly item shapes and symbols.
  • Plays offline.

About High Water Designs

High Water Designs LLC is an independent studio that makes short, fair puzzle games for phones. Restock Rush is its first game.
