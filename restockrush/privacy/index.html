Restock Rush Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026 · Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains what information the Restock Rush mobile game (the "Game") collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. The Game is published by High Water Designs LLC ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy, write to support@highwaterdesigns.com.

The short version: you do not need an account to play, we never ask for your name, email or contacts, and your game progress is stored on your device. To keep the Game free, we show ads and measure how the Game is played. Those features use identifiers from your device, and the partners listed below process that data under their own privacy policies.

1. Information we collect

Stored only on your device

Your game progress (levels completed, stars, coins, boosters, shop renovation, settings) is saved on your device. We do not receive this save file. If you uninstall the Game, it is deleted. Purchases that remove ads are restored through Google Play.

Collected automatically when you play

  • Gameplay and app activity: events such as starting, finishing or failing a level, time spent, boosters and revives used, coins earned and spent, and interactions with ads and the in-game shop.
  • Device and app identifiers: the Android advertising ID (where you have not deleted or disabled it), the app set ID, Firebase installation and app instance IDs, and similar identifiers created by our partners' software.
  • Device and technical information: device model, operating system version, language, screen size, app version, and performance data such as frame rate and memory use.
  • Approximate location: our ad and analytics partners may estimate your country or city from your IP address. We do not collect precise (GPS) location.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: if the Game crashes or stops responding, a report of the device state and the code that failed.
  • Purchase information: which in-app product you bought, its price and currency, and a purchase token used to confirm and restore it. Payment is handled by Google Play; we never receive your card or bank details.

Information you send us

If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message, and use it only to answer you.

We do not collect your name, phone number, contacts, photos, files, messages, microphone, camera or precise location, and the Game has no chat or user accounts.

2. How we use information

  • To run the Game: deliver purchases, restore them, apply settings and fix problems.
  • To improve the Game: understand which levels are too hard or too easy, where players stop playing, and how the Game performs on different phones.
  • To test changes: try different settings (for example level difficulty or how often ads appear) with groups of players and keep what works better.
  • To show ads: serve ads, measure them, cap how often you see them, and, if you allow it, show ads that are more relevant to you.
  • To prevent fraud and keep things secure: detect invalid ad traffic and fraudulent purchases.
  • To meet legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Sharing identifiers with ad partners to show personalized ads may be treated as a "sale" or "sharing" under some US state laws; see section 7 for how to opt out.

4. Advertising and your choices

The Game shows optional rewarded video ads (you choose to watch one for a reward) and, after some levels, full-screen ads between levels. We never show ads while a level is being played. A one-time purchase removes the between-level ads.

  • Consent: if you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before personalized ads or non-essential data processing start. Ads do not load until you have made a choice. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices in the Game.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads on most phones). Once deleted, ad networks receive a string of zeros instead, and ads will be less personalized.
  • Non-personalized ads: if you decline personalized ads, you will still see ads, but they will be based on context (such as the Game itself and your general location) rather than your activity in other apps.

5. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play under the Google Play terms. We receive confirmation of what was bought, the price and a purchase token so we can deliver and restore it. We do not see your payment details.

6. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Performance of a contract: delivering the Game, saving settings and delivering purchases.
  • Legitimate interests: crash reporting, security, fraud prevention, and basic, non-personalized measurement that keeps the Game working and improves it.
  • Consent: personalized advertising, and storing or reading information on your device where the law requires consent. You can withdraw consent at any time in the Game's Settings.
  • Legal obligation: where we must keep or disclose information by law.

7. US state privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia or another state with a privacy law), you may have the right to know what personal information we collect, to access or delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. To opt out, turn off personalized ads in Settings > Privacy choices in the Game, or email us. We honor Global Privacy Control signals where they apply. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Categories of personal information collected in the last 12 months: identifiers (device and advertising IDs), internet or other electronic network activity (gameplay and ad interactions), approximate geolocation, commercial information (purchase history), and inferences used for advertising. Sources: your device and our partners' software in the Game. Recipients: the service providers and ad partners listed in section 3.

8. How long we keep information

  • Analytics events linked to a device identifier: 14 months, then deleted or aggregated.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.
  • Aggregated reports that cannot identify you: as long as needed to run the business.
  • Support emails: up to 24 months after your last message.
  • Ad partners keep data under their own retention policies (see section 3).

9. Your rights and how to delete your data

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, object to or restrict some uses, and withdraw consent. Because the Game has no accounts, we need an identifier to find your data:

  1. Open the Game, go to Settings > Privacy choices, and copy your Player ID.
  2. Email it to support@highwaterdesigns.com with the subject "Data request" and say what you would like us to do.

We will answer within 30 days (45 days where US state law allows). We delete the analytics and crash data linked to that ID and ask our partners to do the same where they act on our behalf. Uninstalling the Game deletes your local save. You can also contact the ad partners directly using the links in section 3. If you are in the EEA or UK and are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher minimum age that applies in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

11. International transfers

We and our partners may process information in the United States and other countries whose data protection laws differ from yours. Where the law requires it, these transfers rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

12. Security

Information sent by the Game to us and our partners is encrypted in transit. Access to our analytics data is limited to the people who run the Game. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the Game before it takes effect.
