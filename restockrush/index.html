A shop sort puzzle for Android

Restock Rush

Sort goods from the delivery belt onto your shelves, match three of a kind to clear them, and fill every customer's order before their patience runs out.

Coming soon on Google Play Pre-register on Google Play Press kit

Free to play, with ads between levels and optional in-app purchases.

How it plays

Three moves, endless little decisions

Grab from the belt

Fresh stock keeps rolling in. Drag it or tap it onto a shelf.

Match three

Line up three of the same item to clear them. Chain clears for a Rush combo.

Serve the counter

Fill each customer's order before they lose patience, and they tip you for it.

Fair by design

Play it your way

Every item has its own shape and symbol as well as its colour, so the whole game works for colourblind players too.

  • No ads in the middle of a level.
  • No level timer and no energy system.
  • Shelves jammed? You get a revive to keep going.
  • Plays offline.
  • Earn stars to renovate your shop: a new counter, awning, floor and signs.
